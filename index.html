Everything your agency needs

Built for agencies, not just creators

From a single creator to a full roster, Lustlab CRM scales with how you work — live inside Fansly and OnlyFans, syncing silently in the background.

Revenue Dashboard

See today, week, and month earnings for every creator at once. PPV sales, tips, and subscriptions broken down separately — auto-refreshed every 30 minutes.

Multi-Account Management

Run unlimited creator accounts side-by-side. Each account gets an isolated browser session. Switch instantly with one click — unread message counts visible on every row in the sidebar.

Chatter Tracking

Know exactly who talked to which fan and when. Assign chatters to conversations, see per-chatter revenue attribution, and track shifts. Your agency's accountability layer, built in.

Scripts & Quick PPV

Save your best-converting message templates with prices pre-attached. Send PPV content from a vault picker directly inside the CRM panel — without leaving the conversation. Sent badges prevent double-sends.

Real-Time Notifications

PPV purchases, tips, and new subscribers surface instantly in the notification bell — per account. Missed sales while the app was closed sync automatically on startup so nothing falls through the cracks.

Team & Role Management

Three role tiers — Admin, Manager, Chatter — each with scoped access. Add team members, set custom colours and avatars, and manage the whole team from one workspace.

Fansly & OnlyFans

A platform switcher in the sidebar flips the entire account list between Fansly and OnlyFans. Each platform has isolated sessions, headers, and API integrations — both managed from the same app.

Multi-Workspace

Running more than one agency? Create multiple isolated workspaces, each with their own creator accounts, team members, and local database. Switch between agencies from the login screen.

Subscriber Insights

Live subscriber counts, new subs today, and fan profiles with total spend. Fan notes sync across your team so every chatter knows the VIPs, spending history, and which content converts best.

Setup in minutes

Your agency, online in minutes

Step 1

Connect Your Accounts

Download the app, create your workspace, and log each creator into their Fansly or OnlyFans account. Sessions stay saved — log in once and stay connected.

Step 2

Add Your Team

Create chatter accounts with role-based access. Set up PPV scripts with prices pre-attached. Your team logs in and starts working from the same shared workspace immediately.

Step 3

Track Everything Live

Revenue, notifications, chatter performance, and subscriber counts update in real-time. See every PPV sale and tip the moment it happens — across all accounts at once.
