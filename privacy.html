Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Lustlab CRM ("we", "our", "the Software") handles information when you use our desktop application. We are committed to being transparent about what data the Software accesses and where it goes.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

Lustlab CRM is a locally-installed desktop application. We do not operate servers that collect, store, or process your personal data, platform credentials, or creator account information. The following data never leaves your device to us:

  • Fansly or OnlyFans login sessions, cookies, or tokens
  • Creator account data, earnings figures, or subscriber information
  • Fan or subscriber messages or content
  • Chatter team member names or shift data
  • PPV scripts or media vault contents

2. Data Stored Locally

All data generated by your use of the Software — including account configurations, chatter records, scripts, and local notification history — is stored in a local SQLite database on your own device. You control this data entirely. Uninstalling the Software removes this data unless you have created a manual backup.

3. Authentication & Cloud Sync

If you use workspace login features (team member accounts), authentication is handled through Supabase. The data stored in the cloud is limited to workspace membership records, role assignments, and team configuration. No creator account data or financial data is stored in the cloud. Supabase's own privacy policy applies to data processed through their service.

4. Third-Party Platform Interactions

The Software opens browser sessions to Fansly and OnlyFans on your behalf. These sessions run on your local device. The relevant platform's own privacy policy governs any data you submit to or receive from those platforms. We do not intercept, log, or transmit that data.

5. Analytics & Crash Reporting

The Software does not currently include any analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting that transmits data to us or any third party. If this changes in a future version, we will update this policy and notify users in advance.

6. Automatic Updates

The Software may check GitHub Releases for available updates. This request includes your IP address and basic system information (OS version, app version), which GitHub may log per their own privacy policy. No personal account data is included in update checks.

7. Children's Privacy

The Software is intended for use by business operators only. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 years of age. If you believe a minor is using the Software, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Your Rights

Because virtually all data is stored locally on your device, you have direct control over it at all times. You can delete your local database at any point. For any cloud-stored workspace data, you may request deletion by contacting us and we will remove it within 30 days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the Software or by updating the effective date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle data, contact us at liamofm@lustlablimited.com.
